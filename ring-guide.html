Wispr Ring Controls

Three buttons, three ways to use each. Press, double press, or hold for about a second.

Keep the side switch on OTC O is off. T is TikTok mode and won't work properly.

By button

Next top

  • press
    Scroll up
  • hold
    EscapeCancels Wispr while it's listening

Middle

  • press
    Start or stop WisprSends ⌃⌥W, your hands-free shortcut
  • double
    EnterSends the message
  • hold
    Nothing

Previous bottom

  • press
    Scroll down
  • hold
    Clear the text box⌘A then Delete. Only works in a text box, and beeps anywhere else. ⌘Z brings the text back.

Dictating a message

  1. Click into the text box, then press middle. Wispr starts listening.
  2. Speak.
  3. Press middle again. Wispr stops and pastes your words.
  4. Read it over, then double press middle to send.

Messed up?

Still talking: hold next to cancel. Nothing gets pasted.

Already pasted: hold previous to clear the box, then start again.

Good to know

Scrolling goes to whatever is under the mouse pointer, not necessarily the window you're typing in.

Ring not responding? Check that F3 shows as connected in Bluetooth settings, then restart the helper in Terminal:

launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/local.wisprring
